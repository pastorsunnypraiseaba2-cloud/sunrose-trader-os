Sunrose Trend Index (STI)
One score (0-100) that summarizes everything else on this page -- width, compression, stretch, energy, and phase, combined. Higher = more of a real trend underway; lower = a quiet or dead market.
Market Lifecycle / Phase
Where the market is right now in its natural cycle: coiling up (Compression), breaking out (Expansion), stretched too far (Overextension), snapping back (Pullback), or settling before the next move (Re-accumulation).
Trend Expectation
A realistic read on how far this move could travel, given how the CPR is currently behaving -- not a guarantee, just an expectation based on similar past setups.
Extreme Price Alert
A warning specifically about the R4/S4 levels -- the outer edges of the expected range. Price reaching these levels has historically come with a higher chance of a pullback or reversal.
Current Position
Where price is sitting right now relative to the CPR levels (e.g. "inside the CPR" or "between R2 and R3").
Distance to Extreme
How far price still has to travel to reach R4 or S4 (or how far past it price already is).
Pullback Probability
The estimated odds of a reversal from exactly where price is right now. Low near the CPR center, higher the further price stretches out.
Overextension
A traffic-light read on whether price has moved too far, too fast: 🟢 Normal, 🟡 Extended (consider partial profits), 🔴 Overextended (a pullback is more likely).
Market Stretch vs. Market Energy vs. Expansion Fuel Remaining
These three often get confused because they sound similar -- but each answers a different question:
• Stretch = how far price has already moved from the CPR center, right now.
• Energy = how much force/momentum is behind the move -- is it pushing hard, or stalling?
• Fuel Remaining = how much room is left before price reaches the target, regardless of momentum.

A move can have low Energy but high Fuel Remaining at the same time -- that just means the move has stalled, but there's still plenty of untouched distance ahead if a fresh push comes. That's not a contradiction; think of a car stopped partway down a long, open road.
Sunrose Market Outlook
A forward-looking, next-24-hours style summary that pulls together everything above into one quick read: bias, expected behaviour, likely target, biggest risk, and the mindset to trade with.

STEP-BY-STEP ANALYSIS
1.
Step 1 -- Higher Timeframe Bias: All three timeframes are independently Bearish. On the Daily, swing highs and swing lows have been descending since the early 2026 peak near 5600, with a dominant descending trendline capping every rally. The 4H shows the same descending sequence from the August high near 4600 through the October rally near 4200 and the November rejection near 4150. The 1H confirms with a sharp impulse lower from 4200 to 3860. Price is trading below the Daily, Weekly and Monthly CPR, confirming bearish open positioning across all periods. Bias is firmly Bearish. Continuation direction is SELL.
2.
Step 2 -- Market Location: The relevant impulse leg for the current move runs from the Previous Week High at 4148.48 down to the current week low at 3860.85, giving a range of 287.63 points. The midpoint (equilibrium) of that leg sits near 4004.67. Current price at 3875 is well below that midpoint, placing price in Discount of this bearish impulse. In a bearish continuation context, Discount means price is already extended in the direction of the trend — institutions would not be buying here in size; they would be looking to sell any pullback back toward equilibrium or the upper portion of the range. The PDH at 3923.15, PDL at 3867.81, PWH at 4148.48 and PWL at 3852.74 are all visible reference points.
3.
Step 3 -- Liquidity Analysis: On the 1H chart, the rally from early November pushed price up through the October swing highs near 4100-4150, sweeping buy-side liquidity (stops above those equal highs) before reversing sharply. That is the against-the-trend sweep required for a bearish continuation — buy-side liquidity above prior swing highs was taken and rejected. Following that sweep, price broke down through 4000, then through 3960 and 3900, confirming the bearish BOS sequence. Below current price, the PDL at 3867.81 was wicked through intraday but has not produced a daily close below it — it is partially tagged but not fully swept on a closing basis. The PWL at 3852.74 remains untouched and is the primary sell-side draw. The liquidity sweep condition is partially satisfied: the buy-side sweep above 4100-4150 is confirmed, but the against-the-trend sweep is somewhat distant in time (early November), which reduces the freshness score slightly.
4.
Step 4 -- Character Shift: The sequence is present. The buy-side sweep above 4100-4150 in early November was followed by a bearish CHoCH through 4000 on a closed candle, then a confirmed bearish BOS through 3960 and subsequently through 3900, both on closed candles visible on the 1H chart. The structure shift from bullish to bearish is valid. Price has not recovered above the CHoCH level at 4000, confirming the shift is holding. The sequence — sweep, CHoCH, BOS — is complete and valid.
5.
Step 5 -- Momentum Quality: The impulse leg from the November 5 high near 4175 down to the November 13-15 lows near 3860 covers approximately 315 points. The move is composed of multiple consecutive bearish candles with large bodies and relatively small upper wicks, indicating strong institutional selling pressure. There are no meaningful bullish engulfing candles within the impulse. The speed of the decline, particularly the drop from 4000 to 3860 over a compressed time window, is consistent with institutional order flow rather than retail selling. Momentum is rated Strong.
6.
Step 6 -- Pullback Quality: The impulse leg used for measurement runs from the impulse high at 4148.48 (PWH, the origin of the bearish move) down to the current low at 3860.85, a range of 287.63 points. Current price at 3875 represents a retracement of approximately 4.9% from the low back toward the high — that is a very shallow bounce, well under the 38% threshold. This classifies as a Shallow Pullback. The entry zone of 3900-3940 would represent a retracement of roughly 13.6% to 27.5% of the impulse range — still firmly within Shallow Pullback territory. This is consistent with strong institutional continuation momentum where sellers are not allowing meaningful retracements before resuming the trend.
